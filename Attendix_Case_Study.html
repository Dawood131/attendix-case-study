Overview

What it is

Attendix is an attendance and workforce management system built for a single company's internal operations. It tracks employee check-ins and check-outs in real time, manages leave requests and approvals, handles payroll, and validates attendance against employee location using a geofenced maps integration, all inside one operations dashboard used daily by admins and employees.

Breakdown

What's built into it

The platform is organized into six modules, each handling a distinct part of day-to-day workforce operations.

01

Operations Dashboard

The dashboard surfaces real-time workforce metrics: total employees, who's present, who's on leave, and total work hours logged, alongside attendance trend charts comparing presence, tardiness, and leave over a selected date range. Notifications sit alongside it, keeping admins aware of attendance events as they happen.

  • Live headcount, presence, and leave metrics
  • Attendance trend charts over a selectable date range
  • Real-time notifications
02

Attendance & Workforce

Employees check in and out with their attendance validated against their real location through the maps integration, so a check-in only counts from an approved site. Leave requests move through a manager approval workflow, and every employee's profile and record history is managed from the same workforce section.

  • Real-time check-in/check-out with live status
  • Geofenced, location-based attendance validation
  • Leave application and manager approval workflow
  • Employee profile and record management
03

Payroll

Payroll ties directly into logged attendance and leave data, breaking salary down into a clear payslip view rather than a flat number, so admins and employees can both see how a figure was calculated.

  • Salary breakdown with a dedicated payslip UI
  • Payroll figures driven by attendance and leave records
04

Messaging

An in-app messaging layer lets teams communicate without leaving the platform, paired with the same notification system used for attendance events.

  • In-app team messaging
  • Shared notification system across the platform
05

Locations & Inventory

Beyond attendance, the operations layer tracks company locations (the sites attendance is geofenced against) and inventory records, giving admins visibility into physical resources alongside workforce data.

  • Location records tied to geofenced attendance
  • Inventory tracking within the same operations layer
06

Administration

An admin layer sits on top of the product: managing admin accounts, the company profile, and security settings, along with feature configuration and CMS-style content management for news and FAQs shown to staff.

  • Admin accounts and company profile management
  • Security settings and feature configuration
  • CMS for News and FAQs
